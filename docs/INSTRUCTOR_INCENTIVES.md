# Instructor Incentive Ideas

Ways to incentivize students and help them earn ETH beyond staking rewards.

## Student Earning Mechanisms (Built-in)

| Mechanism | How students earn | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Staking rewards** | Stake ETH → earn APY over time | Automatic; visible in "Pending Rewards" |
| **Block proposal** | Get selected as proposer → 0.01 ETH | Weighted random; advance epoch to trigger |
| **Attestation** | Attest each epoch → avoid penalties, rewards on withdraw | Penalties for missing; no extra bonus by default |

## Quick Reward Buttons (Instructor Dashboard)

Use the **Incentive Ideas & Quick Rewards** section:

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Reward proposer** | Send ETH to the last block proposer |
| **Reward random** | Send ETH to a randomly selected student |
| **Reward attesters** | Send ETH to up to 5 students who attested this epoch |
| **Reward role completers** | Send ETH to all students who registered a role contract |
| **Reward lowest balance** | Send ETH to the student with the smallest balance |
| **Reward newest** | Send ETH to the most recent participant (from activity feed) |

## Random Incentive Ideas

Click **Roll Random Idea** for suggestions. Examples:

- *Reward the block proposer with a bonus 0.5 ETH — they earned it!*
- *First 3 students to attest this epoch get 0.3 ETH each*
- *Random participation bonus: pick a random student and send 2 ETH*
- *All students who completed their role contract get 0.5 ETH*
- *Underdog bonus: student with lowest balance gets 2 ETH*
- *Cold-call bonus: pick random student to explain a concept. Correct = 1 ETH*
- *Ransomware: First investigator to submit correct attacker address gets 3 ETH*
- *Car Sale: Bonus for mechanic who inspects — 0.5 ETH for honest reporting*

## Manual Incentives (No Button)

- **First to complete X** — Watch the Contract column; first to register gets bonus
- **Voting participation** — Fund everyone who voted
- **Help-a-neighbor** — Observe chat; fund students who help others debug
- **Streak reward** — Reward students with 0 missed attestations

## Tips

1. **Set reward amount** — Use the "Quick reward amount" input (default 0.5 ETH)
2. **Announce in chat** — Post artifacts like `[BOUNTY: First to complete role contract gets 1.5 ETH]`
3. **Vary incentives** — Mix random, merit-based, and participation rewards
4. **Fund by address** — For custom rewards, use the Fund by address field
